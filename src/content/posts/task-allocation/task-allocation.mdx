To train models, AI labs must procure both RL environments and evals targeting the capabilities they care about.
RL environments and evals are mostly interchangeable,
but due to Goodhart's Law one can rarely use the same task for both RL and evaluation.
Labs are constrained on how many tasks they can procure for use during evaluation or RL,
so how should they allocate their limited resources among these two purposes?
